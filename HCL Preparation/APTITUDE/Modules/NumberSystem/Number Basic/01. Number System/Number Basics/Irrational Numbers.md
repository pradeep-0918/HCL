# Irrational Numbers

## 1. What are Irrational Numbers?

An irrational number is a number that **cannot be expressed in the form**:

$$
\frac{p}{q}
$$

where:

$$
p,q\in\mathbb{Z},\quad q\neq0
$$

### Examples

$$
\sqrt2,\quad\sqrt3,\quad\sqrt5,\quad\sqrt7,\quad\pi
$$

These cannot be represented as a fraction of two integers.

---

# 2. Decimal Representation

The most important property for aptitude:

> **Irrational numbers have non-terminating, non-repeating decimal expansions.**

Example:

$$
\sqrt2=1.41421356237\ldots
$$

It continues forever and has no repeating pattern.

Therefore:

[
\boxed{\sqrt2\text{ is irrational}}
]

---

# 3. Rational vs Irrational

| Decimal type                  | Number type |
| ----------------------------- | ----------- |
| Terminating                   | Rational    |
| Non-terminating repeating     | Rational    |
| Non-terminating non-repeating | Irrational  |

### ⭐ Golden Rule

$$
\boxed{\text{Non-terminating + Non-repeating}=\text{Irrational}}
$$

---

# 4. Square Root Shortcut

This is extremely important in aptitude.

If (n) is a **perfect square**:

$$
\sqrt n
$$

is rational.

Examples:

$$
\sqrt4=2
$$

$$
\sqrt9=3
$$

$$
\sqrt{16}=4
$$

Therefore:

[
\boxed{\sqrt4,\sqrt9,\sqrt{16}\text{ are rational}}
]

---

If (n) is **not a perfect square**:

$$
\sqrt n
$$

is irrational.

Examples:

$$
\sqrt2,\sqrt3,\sqrt5,\sqrt6,\sqrt7
$$

Therefore:

[
\boxed{\sqrt2,\sqrt3,\sqrt5\text{ are irrational}}
]

# 7. Simplifying Square Roots

Sometimes a square root looks irrational but contains a perfect-square factor.

Example:

$$
\sqrt{12}
$$

Break 12:

$$
12=4\times3
$$

Therefore:

$$
\sqrt{12}
=========

\sqrt4\sqrt3
$$

$$
=2\sqrt3
$$

# 8. Rational ± Irrational

If (r) is rational and (x) is irrational:

$$
r+x
$$

is irrational.

Similarly:

$$
r-x
$$

is irrational.

Example:

$$
5+\sqrt2
$$

is irrational.

---

# 9. Rational × Irrational

If (r) is a **non-zero rational number** and (x) is irrational:

$$
r\times x
$$

is irrational.

Example:

$$
3\sqrt2
$$

is irrational.

### ⚠️ Important exception

[
0\times\sqrt2=0
]

and (0) is rational.

So the rational number must be **non-zero**.

---

# 10. Irrational + Irrational

This is a common trick question.

The result can be **rational OR irrational**.

### Example producing rational:

$$
\sqrt2+(-\sqrt2)=0
$$

0 is rational.

### Example producing irrational:

$$
\sqrt2+\sqrt3
$$

is irrational.
