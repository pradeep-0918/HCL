# Rational Numbers

## 1. What are Rational Numbers?

A rational number is any number that can be written in the form:

$$  
\boxed{\frac{p}{q}}  
$$

where:

$$  
p,q\in\mathbb{Z}  
$$

and:

$$  
\boxed{q\neq0}  
$$

### Examples

$$  
\frac12,\quad\frac35,\quad\frac{-7}{4},\quad5,\quad0  
$$

Why are (5) and (0) rational?

Because they can be written as:

$$  
5=\frac51  
$$

$$  
0=\frac01  
$$

---

# 2. The Most Important Condition

For:

$$  
\frac{p}{q}  
$$

the denominator **cannot be zero**.

### ⭐ Remember

> **Zero numerator → allowed**  
> **Zero denominator → not allowed**

---

# 3. Examples of Rational Numbers

### Fractions

$$  
\frac23,\quad\frac{-5}{8}  
$$

### Integers

$$  
7=\frac71  
$$

$$  
-4=\frac{-4}{1}  
$$

### Zero

$$  
0=\frac01  
$$

### Terminating Decimals

$$  
0.5=\frac12  
$$

$$  
2.75=\frac{11}{4}  
$$

Therefore terminating decimals are rational.

---

# 4. Recurring Decimals

A recurring/repeating decimal is also rational.

Example:

$$  
0.3333\ldots  
$$

This can be written as:

$$  
\frac13  
$$

Therefore:

Another example:

$$  
0.121212\ldots  
$$

is rational.

---

# 5. Terminating vs Non-Terminating Decimals

### Terminating Decimal

A decimal that ends.

Example:

$$  
0.25  
$$


### Non-Terminating Recurring

A decimal that continues but repeats.

Example:

$$  
0.6666\ldots  
$$


### Non-Terminating Non-Recurring

A decimal that continues forever without repeating.

Example:

$$  
\pi=3.14159265\ldots  
$$

| Operation      | Rational Numbers               |
| -------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Addition       | ✅ Closed                       |
| Subtraction    | ✅ Closed                       |
| Multiplication | ✅ Closed                       |
| Division       | ✅ Closed, except division by 0 |

# 8. Simplest Form

A rational number is usually written in its lowest form.

Example:

$$  
\frac{12}{18}  
$$

HCF of 12 and 18:

$$  
6  
$$

Divide both by 6:

$$  
\frac{12}{18}=\frac23  
$$

Therefore:



---

# 9. Equivalent Rational Numbers

Different fractions can represent the same value.

Example:

$$  
\frac12=\frac24=\frac36=\frac5{10}  
$$

These are called **equivalent fractions**.

### Shortcut

Multiply numerator and denominator by the same non-zero number.
