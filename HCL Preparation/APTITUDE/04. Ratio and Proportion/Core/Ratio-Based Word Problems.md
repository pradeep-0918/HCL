# Ratio-Based Word Problems

## 1. Core Concept

> [!summary]
> Ratio word problems describe the relationship between quantities using `:`.

The main idea is:

$$
\boxed{A:B=m:n}
$$

This means:

$$
A=mk
$$

and:

$$
B=nk
$$

where `k` is the **common multiplier**.

> **Ratio → Parts → One Part → Actual Values**

---

# 2. Basic Interpretation

If:

$$
A:B=2:3
$$

then:

- A = 2 parts
- B = 3 parts
- Total = 5 parts

If total = `50`:

$$
1\text{ part}=\frac{50}{5}=10
$$

Therefore:

$$
A=2(10)=20
$$

$$
B=3(10)=30
$$

Answer:

$$
\boxed{A=20,\ B=30}
$$

---

# 3. Golden Method

> [!important]
> For most ratio word problems:

### Step 1
Write the ratio.

### Step 2
Add the ratio parts if total is given.

### Step 3
Find one part.

### Step 4
Multiply by the required ratio part.

### Step 5
Check the answer.

---

# 4. Total Given

A:B = `3:5`.

Total = `64`.

Total parts:

$$
3+5=8
$$

One part:

$$
64/8=8
$$

Therefore:

$$
A=3(8)=24
$$

$$
B=5(8)=40
$$

Answer:

$$
\boxed{24,\ 40}
$$

---

# 5. Difference Given

A:B = `3:5`.

Difference = `20`.

Difference in parts:

$$
5-3=2
$$

Therefore:

$$
2k=20
$$

$$
k=10
$$

A:

$$
3k=30
$$

B:

$$
5k=50
$$

Answer:

$$
\boxed{30,\ 50}
$$

---

# 6. Total vs Difference

> [!important]

### If total is given:

$$
\boxed{k=\frac{\text{Total}}{\text{Sum of parts}}}
$$

### If difference is given:

$$
\boxed{k=\frac{\text{Difference}}{\text{Difference of parts}}}
$$

# 9. Ratio and Total Formula

If:

$$
A:B=m:n
$$

and:

$$
A+B=T
$$

then:

$$
\boxed{
A=\frac{m}{m+n}T
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
B=\frac{n}{m+n}T
}
$$

---

# 10. Ratio and Difference Formula

If:

$$
A:B=m:n
$$

and:

$$
B-A=D
$$

then:

$$
\boxed{
A=\frac{mD}{n-m}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
B=\frac{nD}{n-m}
}
$$

for `n > m`.

A:B = `2:3`.

Suppose `10` is added to both quantities and the new ratio becomes `3:4`.

$$

$$

$$

$$

$$
k=10

$$
\boxed{20,\ 30}

# 51. Ratio and Product

If:

$$
A:B=m:n
$$

and:

$$
AB=P
$$

then:

$$
A=mk
$$

$$
B=nk
$$

Therefore:

$$
mnk^2=P
$$

So:

$$
\boxed{
k=\sqrt{\frac{P}{mn}}
}
$$

Example:

A:B = `2:3`, product = `150`.

$$
6k^2=150
$$

$$
k^2=25
$$

$$
k=5
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{A=10,\ B=15}
$$

# 65. Common Mistakes

> [!warning] Avoid These

### Mistake 1 — Adding Ratio Terms Directly to Quantities

If:

$$
A:B=2:3
$$

do not assume:

$$
A=2,\ B=3
$$

They are:

$$
A=2k,\ B=3k
$$

---

### Mistake 2 — Forgetting Total Parts

For:

$$
2:3
$$

total parts:

$$
\boxed{5}
$$

---

### Mistake 3 — Using Difference Instead of Sum

If total is given:

$$
m+n
$$

If difference is given:

$$
n-m
$$

---

### Mistake 4 — Not Equalizing Common Terms

For:

$$
A:B=2:3
$$

and:

$$
B:C=4:5
$$

you cannot directly write:

$$
A:B:C=2:3:5
$$

because B is `3` in one ratio and `4` in the other.

---

### Mistake 5 — Confusing Ratio With Percentage

`2:3` does not mean `2%:3%`.

---

### Mistake 6 — Ignoring Units

Always convert:

- kg ↔ g
- m ↔ cm
- hours ↔ minutes
- ₹ ↔ paise

before forming ratios.

> [!summary] One-Minute Revision

### Ratio

$$
A:B=2:3
$$

means:

$$
\boxed{A=2k,\ B=3k}
$$

### Total = 50

$$
5k=50
$$

$$
k=10
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{A=20,\ B=30}
$$

### Difference = 10

$$
3k-2k=10
$$

$$
k=10
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{A=20,\ B=30}
$$

### Three-Term Ratio

$$
A:B:C=2:3:5
$$

Total parts:

$$
\boxed{10}
$$

$$
A:B=2:3
$$

$$
B:C=4:5
$$

Equalize B:

$$
\boxed{A:B:C=8:12:15}
$$

### Golden Memory Trick

> **Ratio = Parts.  
> Total = Sum of Parts.  
> Difference = Difference of Parts.  
> One Part = Given ÷ Relevant Parts.**

### One-Line Recognition

> **Whenever a word problem gives a ratio, convert every quantity into `ratio part × common multiplier (k)` before solving.**
