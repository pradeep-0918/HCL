# Simplification of Ratios

## 1. Core Concept

> [!summary]
> **Simplifying a ratio** means reducing it to its smallest equivalent form.

For:

$$
a:b
$$

divide both terms by their **HCF/GCD**.

$$
\boxed{
a:b
=
\frac{a}{HCF}:\frac{b}{HCF}
}
$$

---

# 2. Basic Example

Simplify:

$$
24:36
$$

HCF:

$$
HCF(24,36)=12
$$

Divide both terms by `12`:

$$
24:36
=
2:3
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{2:3}
$$

---

# 3. Why HCF?

The HCF is the largest number that divides both terms.

Therefore, dividing by HCF produces the **smallest integer ratio**.

Example:

$$
36:48
$$

HCF:

$$
12
$$

Therefore:

$$
36:48
=
\boxed{3:4}
$$

---

# 4. Simplification Rule

> [!important]
> **Always perform the same operation on both terms.**

Correct:

$$
20:30
\div10
=
2:3
$$

Incorrect:

$$
20:30
\rightarrow
2:30
$$

---

# 5. Simplifying by Common Factors

Simplify:

$$
45:60
$$

Both divisible by `15`:

$$
45\div15=3
$$

$$
60\div15=4
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{3:4}
$$

---

# 6. Prime Factor Method

Simplify:

$$
84:126
$$

Prime factors:

$$
84=2^2\times3\times7
$$

$$
126=2\times3^2\times7
$$

Common factors:

$$
2\times3\times7=42
$$

Therefore:

$$
84:126
=
2:3
$$

Answer:

$$
\boxed{2:3}
$$

---

# 7. Euclidean Algorithm Method

For large numbers, use HCF.

Example:

$$
252:378
$$

HCF:

$$
378-252=126
$$

Since:

$$
252\div126=2
$$

$$
378\div126=3
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{2:3}
$$

# 22. Negative Values

\boxed{2:3}

But if one quantity is negative and the other positive, interpret the question carefully.

# 47. Simplification and HCF

For integer ratios:

$$
a:b
$$

the simplest ratio is obtained by:

$$
\boxed{
\frac{a}{\gcd(a,b)}
:
\frac{b}{\gcd(a,b)}
}
$$

---

# 48. Fast HCF Trick

For two numbers:

$$
a:b
$$

if both are divisible by `2`, divide by `2`.

If both remain divisible by `3`, divide by `3`.

Continue until no common factor remains.

Example:

$$
72:108
$$

Divide by `2`:

$$
36:54
$$

Divide by `2`:

$$
18:27
$$

Divide by `9`:

$$
\boxed{2:3}
$$

---

# 49. Prime Factor Cancellation

$$

$$

Prime factors:

$$
180=2^2\times3^2\times5
$$

$$
252=2^2\times3^2\times7
$$

Common:

$$
2^2\times3^2=36
$$

Remaining:

$$
5:7
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{5:7}
$$

Suppose:

Boys : Girls:

$$
36:48
$$

Simplified:

$$
3:4
$$

Total parts:

$$
3+4=7
$$

Total students:

$$
84
$$

One part:

$$
84/7=12
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{36\text{ boys},\ 48\text{ girls}}
$$

# 54. Simplification Does Not Change Relationship

Suppose:

$$
A:B=60:90
$$

Simplify:

$$
2:3
$$

The relationship has not changed.

Only the scale has changed.

# 55. Common Mistakes

> [!warning] Avoid These

### Mistake 1 — Different Units

Wrong:

$$
2m:50cm=2:50
$$

Correct:

$$
200:50
=
\boxed{4:1}
$$

### Mistake 2 — Dividing Only One Term

Wrong:

$$
20:30\rightarrow2:30
$$

### Mistake 3 — Not Fully Simplifying

$$
12:18
$$

should become:

$$
\boxed{2:3}
$$

### Mistake 4 — Forgetting Powers

Side ratio `2:3` does not mean area ratio `2:3`.

Area ratio:

$$
\boxed{4:9}
$$

### Mistake 5 — Treating Different Units as Different Quantities

Convert first.

# 57. Formula Sheet

> [!important] Must Remember

### Integer Ratio

$$
\boxed{
a:b
\rightarrow
\frac a{\gcd(a,b)}:
\frac b{\gcd(a,b)}
}
$$

### Fraction Ratio

$$
\boxed{
\frac ab:\frac cd
=
ad:bc
}
$$

### Equivalent Ratio

$$
\boxed{
a:b=ka:kb
}
$$

### Proportion Test

$$
\boxed{
a:b=c:d
\iff
ad=bc
}
$$

### Square Ratio

If:

$$
a:b=m:n
$$

then:

$$
\boxed{
a^2:b^2=m^2:n^2
}
$$

### Cube Ratio

$$
\boxed{
a^3:b^3=m^3:n^3
}
$$
