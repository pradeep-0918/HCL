# Triplicate Ratio

## 1. Core Concept

> [!summary]
> The **triplicate ratio** of `a:b` is the ratio of their cubes.

If:

$$
a:b
$$

then:

$$
\boxed{a^3:b^3}
$$

### Golden Rule

> **Triplicate = Cube both terms.**

---

# 2. Basic Example

Given:

$$
2:3
$$

Cube both terms:

$$
2^3:3^3
$$

$$
8:27
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{8:27}
$$

---

# 3. Formula

If:

$$
a:b
$$

then:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Triplicate Ratio}=a^3:b^3
}
$$

or:

$$
\boxed{
(a:b)^3=a^3:b^3
}
$$

---

# 4. Examples

### `3:4`

$$
3^3:4^3
$$

$$
\boxed{27:64}
$$

### `4:5`

$$
4^3:5^3
$$

$$
\boxed{64:125}
$$

### `5:7`

$$
5^3:7^3
$$

$$
\boxed{125:343}
$$

---

# 5. Why Is It Called Triplicate?

A ratio compounded with itself three times gives:

$$
(a:b)(a:b)(a:b)
$$

Therefore:

$$
a^3:b^3
$$

Hence:

$$
\boxed{\text{Triplicate Ratio}=(a:b)^3}
$$

---

# 6. Connection With Compound Ratio

Compound ratio:

$$
(a:b)(c:d)=ac:bd
$$

If all three ratios are the same:

$$
(a:b)(a:b)(a:b)
$$

then:

$$
\boxed{a^3:b^3}
$$

Therefore:

> **Triplicate ratio is a special case of compound ratio.**

---

# 7. Duplicate vs Triplicate

| Ratio Type | Operation | `2:3` |
|---|---|---|
| Original | Power 1 | `2:3` |
| Duplicate | Power 2 | `4:9` |
| Triplicate | Power 3 | `8:27` |

> [!important]
> Do not confuse **triplicate** with multiplying both terms by `3`.

---

# 8. Triplicate Does NOT Mean ×3

Given:

$$
2:5
$$

Wrong:

$$
6:15
$$

That is an equivalent ratio.

Correct triplicate:

$$
2^3:5^3
$$

$$
\boxed{8:125}
$$

---

# 9. Simplify Before Cubing

Suppose:

$$
6:9
$$

Simplify first:

$$
2:3
$$

Then cube:

$$
2^3:3^3
$$

$$
\boxed{8:27}
$$

If cubed first:

$$
216:729
$$

Simplifying gives:

$$
\boxed{8:27}
$$

> [!tip]
> **Simplify first whenever possible.**

---

# 10. Finding Original Ratio

Suppose the triplicate ratio is:

$$
8:27
$$

Take cube roots:

$$
\sqrt[3]{8}:\sqrt[3]{27}
$$

$$
\boxed{2:3}
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Original Ratio}=
\sqrt[3]{8}:\sqrt[3]{27}
}
$$

---

# 11. Example

# 19. Algebraic Ratio

If:

$$
x:y=2:5
$$

then:

$$
x^3:y^3
=
2^3:5^3
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{8:125}
$$

---

# 20. General Power Rule

If:

$$
x:y=a:b
$$

then:

$$
\boxed{
x^n:y^n=a^n:b^n
}
$$

Therefore:

- `n = 1` → original ratio
- `n = 2` → duplicate ratio
- `n = 3` → triplicate ratio

---

# 21. Triplicate Ratio and Cubes

If:

$$
x:y=3:4
$$

then:

$$
x^3:y^3
=
3^3:4^3
$$

$$
\boxed{27:64}
$$

This pattern is extremely common in aptitude and geometry questions.

---

# 22. Geometry — Volume

Volume of a cube:

$$
V=a^3
$$

If side ratio is:

$$
2:3
$$

then volume ratio is:

$$
2^3:3^3
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{8:27}
$$

---

# 23. Sphere Volume

Volume of a sphere:

$$
V=\frac43\pi r^3
$$

Therefore:

$$
V_1:V_2=r_1^3:r_2^3
$$

If:

$$
r_1:r_2=2:3
$$

then:

$$
V_1:V_2
=
8:27
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{8:27}
$$

---

# 24. Cube Volume

# 25. Cuboid With All Dimensions Scaled

Suppose two similar cuboids have corresponding dimensions in ratio:

$$

$$

$$

$$

$$
\boxed{8:27}
$$

> This works when **all corresponding linear dimensions** have the same ratio.

$$

$$

=
\boxed{8:27}
$$

Remember:

> **Area → square. Volume → cube.**

# 28. Finding Unknown Using Triplicate Ratio

Suppose the triplicate ratio of:

$$
x:5
$$

is:

$$
64:125
$$

Then:

$$
x^3:5^3
=
64:125
$$

Therefore:

$$
x^3=64
$$

$$
\boxed{x=4}
$$

---

# 29. Another Unknown Example

The triplicate ratio of:

$$
3:x
$$

is:

$$
27:125
$$

Therefore:

$$
3^3:x^3=27:125
$$

$$
x^3=125
$$

$$
\boxed{x=5}
$$

---

# 30. Triplicate Ratio and Proportion

If:

$$
a:b=c:d
$$

then cubing both ratios gives:

$$
a^3:b^3=c^3:d^3
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
a^3:b^3=c^3:d^3
}
$$

---

# 31. Example

Given:

$$
2:3=8:12
$$

Cube both sides:

$$
8:27
=
512:1728
$$

Simplify:

$$
512:1728
=
8:27
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{8:27=8:27}
$$

---

# 32. Triplicate Ratio and Comparison

For positive ratios, if:

$$
a:b>c:d
$$

then:

$$
\boxed{
a^3:b^3>c^3:d^3
}
$$

Example:

$$
2:3>1:2
$$

because:

$$
\frac23>\frac12
$$

Triplicate:

$$
8:27>1:8
$$

---

# 33. Subtriplicate Ratio

The reverse operation is called the **subtriplicate ratio**.

If:

$$
a:b
$$

then:

$$
\boxed{
\sqrt[3]a:\sqrt[3]b
}
$$

Example:

$$
8:27
$$

Subtriplicate:

$$
\sqrt[3]8:\sqrt[3]{27}
$$

$$
\boxed{2:3}
$$

---

# 34. Triplicate vs Subtriplicate

### Triplicate

Cube:

$$
a:b
\rightarrow
\boxed{a^3:b^3}
$$

### Subtriplicate

Cube root:

$$
a:b
\rightarrow
\boxed{\sqrt[3]a:\sqrt[3]b}
$$

They are inverse operations for positive quantities.

---

# 35. Triplicate Ratio and Compound Ratio

Triplicate:

$$
(a:b)^3
$$

means:

$$
(a:b)(a:b)(a:b)
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
(a:b)^3=a^3:b^3
}
$$

This is exactly a compound ratio formed from three identical ratios.

---

# 36. Important Shortcut

If the original ratio is already simple:

$$
2:3
$$

$$
\boxed{8:27}
$$

| 4 | 64 |
| 5 | 125 |
| 6 | 216 |

# 44. Triplicate Ratio and Percentage

Suppose:

$$

# 45. Common Mistakes

> [!warning] Avoid These

### Mistake 1 — Multiply by 3

Wrong:

$$
2:5\rightarrow6:15
$$

Correct:

$$
\boxed{8:125}
$$

---

### Mistake 2 — Square Instead of Cube

Wrong:

$$
2:3\rightarrow4:9
$$

That is duplicate ratio.

Correct:

$$
\boxed{8:27}
$$

---

### Mistake 3 — Forgetting to Simplify

$$
6^3:9^3
=
216:729
$$

Simplify:

$$
\boxed{8:27}
$$

---

### Mistake 4 — Applying Cube When Relationship Is Linear

If:

$$
A\propto x
$$

use the original ratio.

If:

$$
A\propto x^3
$$

use the triplicate ratio.
