# Fraction to Percentage

## 1. Core Concept

> [!summary] Definition
> To convert a fraction into a percentage, multiply the fraction by `100%`.

The fundamental formula is:

$$
\boxed{
\frac{a}{b}\times100\%
}
$$

Example:

$$
\frac{3}{4}\times100\%
$$

$$
=75\%
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{\frac34=75\%}
$$

---

# 2. Why It Works

We know:

$$
1=\frac{100}{100}=100\%
$$

Therefore:

$$
\frac{a}{b}
=
\frac{a}{b}\times\frac{100}{100}
$$

The numerator becomes the percentage value:

$$
\boxed{
\frac ab
=
\frac{100a}{b}\%
}
$$

---

# 3. Basic Formula

For any fraction:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Percentage}
=
\frac{\text{Numerator}}{\text{Denominator}}\times100
}
$$

Example:

$$
\frac{7}{20}
$$

$$
=\frac7{20}\times100
$$

$$
=\boxed{35\%}
$$

---

# 4. Shortcut When Denominator Is 100

# 36. Fraction to Percentage Using Division

Another method:

$$
\frac ab
$$

$$
a\div b
$$

Then multiply by `100`.

Example:

$$
\frac{7}{16}
$$

$$
7\div16=0.4375
$$

Then:

$$
0.4375\times100
=
\boxed{43.75\%}
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{43.75\%}

# 48. Fast Conversion Strategy

> [!tip] Exam Method

When converting:

$$
\frac ab
$$

to percentage:

### Step 1

Check whether the fraction can be simplified.

### Step 2

Check whether `b` can easily become `100`.

### Step 3

If yes, multiply numerator and denominator.

### Step 4

If not, convert to decimal or divide `100` by the denominator.

### Step 5

Multiply the numerator by that factor.

# 57. Important Trap — Percentage of What?

Consider:

> `20 is what percentage of 50?`

The denominator is `50`.

$$
\frac{20}{50}\times100
=
\boxed{40\%}
$$

Never reverse the fraction accidentally.

Wrong:

$$
\frac{50}{20}\times100
$$

---

# 58. Important Trap — Fraction vs Percentage

These are different:

$$
\frac14
$$

and:

$$
\frac14\%
$$

The first is:

$$
\boxed{25\%}
$$

The second is:

$$
\frac{1}{4\times100}\times100\%
=
\boxed{0.25\%}
$$

> [!warning]
> The `%` symbol changes the meaning significantly.

---

$$

$$
100\%
=

\boxed1

$$
100\%\text{ of }500
=

# 61. Important Pattern — Numerator Changes

For the same denominator:

$$
\frac{a}{n}
$$

a change in the numerator directly changes the percentage.

Example:

$$
\frac3{20}=15\%
$$

$$
\frac4{20}=20\%
$$

Difference:

$$
\boxed5\text{ percentage points}}
$$

---

# 62. Important Pattern — Denominator Changes

Changing the denominator changes the percentage even when the numerator remains the same.

Example:

$$
\frac5{10}=50\%
$$

but:

$$
\frac5{20}=25\%
$$

Therefore:

> [!warning]
> Never compare fractions based only on their numerators.

---

# 63. Percentage From Ratio

If:

$$
A:B=3:7
$$

then A as a percentage of B:

$$
\frac37\times100
$$

$$
=\boxed{42\frac67\%}
$$

If A as a percentage of total:

$$
\frac3{3+7}\times100
$$

$$
=\boxed{30\%}
$$

> [!important]
> Always identify whether the question asks for percentage **of the other quantity** or percentage **of the total**.

---

# 64. Part as Percentage of Total

If quantities are in ratio:

$$
A:B:C=2:3:5
$$

Total parts:

$$
2+3+5=10
$$

Percentage of A:

$$
\frac2{10}\times100
=
\boxed{20\%}
$$

Percentage of B:

$$
\frac3{10}\times100
=
\boxed{30\%}
$$

Percentage of C:

$$
\frac5{10}\times100
=
\boxed{50\%}
$$
