# Decimal to Percentage

## 1. Core Concept

> [!summary] Definition
> To convert a decimal into a percentage, **multiply the decimal by `100` and attach `%`**.

$$
\boxed{
\text{Percentage}=\text{Decimal}\times100
}
$$

Example:

$$
0.25\times100
=
\boxed{25\%}
$$

---

# 2. Why It Works

We know:

$$
1=100\%
$$

Therefore:

$$
0.25=25\%
$$

More generally:

$$
\boxed{
x=100x\%
}
$$

Example:

$$
0.72=72\%
$$

---

# 3. Main Conversion Rule

$$
\boxed{
\text{Decimal}\rightarrow\text{Percentage}
=
\text{Decimal}\times100
}
$$

Example:

$$
0.48\times100
=
\boxed{48\%}
$$

---

# 4. Decimal Less Than 1

For decimals between `0` and `1`:

$$
0<x<1
$$

the percentage lies between:

$$
0\%<100x<100\%
$$

Example:

$$
0.63
=
\boxed{63\%}
$$

---

# 5. Decimal Equal to 1

$$
1\times100
=
\boxed{100\%}
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{1=100\%}
$$

---

# 38. Decimal to Percentage vs Percentage to Decimal

These operations are opposites.

### Decimal → Percentage

Multiply by `100`:

$$
\boxed{
x\rightarrow100x\%
}
$$

### Percentage → Decimal

Divide by `100`:

$$
\boxed{
x\%\rightarrow\frac{x}{100}
}
$$

---

# 39. Conversion Cycle

You should be able to move between all three forms:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Fraction}
\leftrightarrow
\text{Decimal}
\leftrightarrow
\text{Percentage}
}
$$

Example:

$$
\frac14
=
0.25
=
25\%
$$

Another:

$$
\frac35
=
0.6
=
60\%
$$

# 40. Important Pattern — Decimal Places

When converting decimal to percentage:

> **Move the decimal point two places right.**

Example:

$$
0.037
\rightarrow
3.7
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{0.037=3.7\%}
$$

---

# 41. Important Pattern — Add Zeros

If necessary, add zeros before shifting.

Example:

$$
0.006
$$

Write:

$$
0.0060
$$

Move two places:

$$
0.60
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{0.006=0.6\%}
$$

---

# 42. Important Pattern — Decimal Greater Than 1

For:

$$
x>1
$$

the percentage is:

$$
\boxed{x\times100\%}
$$

Example:

$$
1.08
=
108\%
$$

This means the value is `8%` greater than the original if `1.08` is being used as a multiplier.

# 43. Important Trap — `0.5%` vs `0.5`

These are very different.

$$
0.5=50\%
$$

But:

$$
0.5\%=0.005
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
0.5\ne0.5\%
}
$$

# 53. Formula Sheet

> [!important] Must Remember

### Main Formula

$$
\boxed{
\text{Percentage}
=
\text{Decimal}\times100
}
$$

### Decimal → Percentage

$$
\boxed{
x\rightarrow100x\%
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
x\%\rightarrow\frac{x}{100}
}
$$

### Multiplier → Percentage

$$
\boxed{
m\times100\%
}
$$

### Increase From Multiplier

If multiplier is `m`:

$$
\boxed{
(m-1)\times100\%
}
$$

for `m > 1`.

### Decrease From Multiplier

If multiplier is `m`:

$$
\boxed{
(1-m)\times100\%
}
$$

for `0<m<1`.

# 58. Quick Revision

> [!summary] One-Minute Revision

### Main Rule

$$
\boxed{
\text{Decimal}\times100=\text{Percentage}
}
$$

### Fastest Method

> **Move the decimal point 2 places to the right and add `%`.**

Examples:

$$
0.25\rightarrow25\%
$$

$$
0.625\rightarrow62.5\%
$$

$$
1.25\rightarrow125\%
$$

### Multiplier

$$
\boxed{
1.2=120\%=20\%\text{ increase}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
0.8=80\%=20\%\text{ decrease}
}
$$

### Golden Memory Trick

> **Decimal → Percentage: move 2 places right. Percentage → Decimal: move 2 places left.**

### One-Line Recognition

> **Whenever a decimal represents a fraction of a whole, multiplying it by `100` immediately gives its percentage representation.**
