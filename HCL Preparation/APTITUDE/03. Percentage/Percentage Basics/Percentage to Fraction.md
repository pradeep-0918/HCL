# Percentage to Fraction

## 1. Core Concept

> [!summary] Definition
> To convert a percentage into a fraction, **remove the `%` symbol and divide the number by `100`**.

The fundamental formula is:

$$
\boxed{
x\%=\frac{x}{100}
}
$$

Example:

$$
25\%
=
\frac{25}{100}
=
\boxed{\frac14}
$$

---

# 2. Why It Works

By definition:

$$
1\%=\frac1{100}
$$

Therefore:

$$
x\%=x\times\frac1{100}
$$

Hence:

$$
\boxed{
x\%=\frac{x}{100}
}
$$

---

# 3. Basic Conversion Rule

To convert:

$$
x\%
$$

into a fraction:

### Step 1

Remove `%`.

### Step 2

Put the number over `100`.

### Step 3

Simplify.

Example:

$$
40\%
=
\frac{40}{100}
=
\boxed{\frac25}
$$

Converting percentage to fraction gives the multiplier directly.

Example:

$$

`20% of N` means:

$$
\boxed{\frac N5}

# 44. Reverse Percentage Connection

Suppose after a `25%` increase, the final value is `500`.

The increase multiplier is:

$$
125\%
=
\frac54
$$

Therefore:

$$
\text{Original}\times\frac54=500
$$

So:

$$
\text{Original}
=
500\times\frac45
$$

$$
=\boxed{400}
$$

This is the foundation of reverse percentage problems.

# 45. Percentage Comparison Using Fractions

$$
A=75\%

$$

$$

$$

$$

$$
\frac34>\frac12
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{75\%>50\%}
$$

# 46. Fraction of Percentage

Example:

Find:

$$
\frac35\text{ of }40\%
$$

Since:

$$
40\%=\frac25
$$

then:

$$
\frac35\times\frac25
=
\frac6{25}
$$

Convert back:

$$
\frac6{25}\times100
=
\boxed{24\%}
$$

# 47. Percentage of Fraction

Example:

Find `20%` of:

$$
\frac34
$$

Convert:

$$
20\%=\frac15
$$

Then:

$$
\frac15\times\frac34
=
\boxed{\frac3{20}}
$$

As a percentage:

$$
\frac3{20}=15\%
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{20\%\text{ of }\frac34=15\%}
$$

# 48. Important Trap — Don't Forget `100`

Wrong:

$$
25\%=\frac{25}{10}
$$

Correct:

$$
\boxed{
25\%=\frac{25}{100}=\frac14
}
$$

---

# 49. Important Trap — Simplify Completely

Example:

$$
60\%
=
\frac{60}{100}
$$

This is correct but not the simplest form.

Simplify:

$$
\frac{60}{100}
=
\boxed{\frac35}
$$

> [!important]
> In aptitude, prefer the **simplest fraction**.

---

# 50. Important Trap — `0.25%`

Do not confuse:

$$
25\%
=
\frac14
$$

with:

$$
0.25\%
=
\frac1{400}
$$

The decimal point changes the answer significantly.

---

# 51. Important Trap — `125%`

$$
125\%
=
\frac54
$$

not:

$$
\frac{125}{1000}
$$

The denominator is always `100` when directly converting a percentage.
