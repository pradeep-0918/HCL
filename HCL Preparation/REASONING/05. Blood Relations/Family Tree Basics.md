# Family Tree Basics

## 1. Core Concept

> [!summary]
> A **Family Tree** is a visual representation of relationships between family members. Blood-relation questions become easier when relationships are converted into a small tree instead of being solved mentally.

The basic idea is:

$$
\boxed{\text{Read Relationship} \rightarrow \text{Draw Family Tree} \rightarrow \text{Identify Generations} \rightarrow \text{Find Answer}}
$$

A family tree represents:

- Parents
- Children
- Grandparents
- Grandchildren
- Brothers
- Sisters
- Uncles
- Aunts
- Cousins
- Nephews
- Nieces

The most important skill is not memorizing relationship names.

It is:

$$
\boxed{\text{Building the correct relationship structure}}
$$
